# Solution — Problem 8: Combined von Neumann Scenario

**(a)** $E_{\text{vN}} = 1000 / (20 \cdot 5 \cdot 2) = 1000 / 200 = 5$ energy units.

**(b)** Efficiency multiplier: $200\times$ (combined effect of all factors).

**(c)** Time = 8 hours / 200 = 0.04 hours ≈ 2.4 minutes.

**(d)** The model suggests most of the advantage is **developed**:
- Compiled intuition ($5\times$) comes from thousands of hours of practice
- Abstraction level ($20\times$) is learned through exposure to advanced mathematics
- Representation switching ($S_{pq} \approx 0$) develops through cross-domain training

Only a small fraction (perhaps the ability to reach $L=4$ and the speed of switching) might have a genetic component. The model thus predicts that deliberate practice at higher abstraction levels is the primary driver of von Neumann-level performance.
