# E&M — Special: Electric Field & Force — Feel Builder

> **Purpose:** Five intuition-building problems on **Coulomb's law** and the **electric field** ($E=F/q$) — tuned for someone who just finished mechanics (think of $E$ as the electric cousin of $g$).
> **Level:** Regular Physics / SAT Physics — **algebraic only, no calculus.**
> **How to use:** Solve the folded sub-problems in order. Full solutions (✅ Solution) live in [`solutions/1-solutions.md`](solutions/1-solutions.md) — open a problem's solution only after finishing it.
> **The core feel to build:** electric force obeys the **inverse square law** (like gravity), and the field $E$ is "force per unit charge" — a property of the space, not of the test charge.
