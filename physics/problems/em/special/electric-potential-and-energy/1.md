# E&M — Special: Electric Potential & Energy — Feel Builder

> **Purpose:** Five intuition-building problems on **electric potential** ($V=U/q$) and **potential energy** — tuned for someone who just finished mechanics (think of $V$ as "electric height").
> **Level:** Regular Physics / SAT Physics — **algebraic only, no calculus.**
> **How to use:** Solve the folded sub-problems in order. Full solutions (✅ Solution) live in [`solutions/1-solutions.md`](solutions/1-solutions.md) — open a problem's solution only after finishing it.
> **The core feel to build:** potential $V$ is **energy per unit charge** — like height per unit mass. Charges gain kinetic energy by "falling" to lower potential, exactly like a ball rolling downhill.
