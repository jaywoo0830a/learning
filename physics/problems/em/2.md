## Problem 1 — Four Charges, Quiet Center *(E and V by symmetry)*

Charges $+2.0\,\mu$C, $+2.0\,\mu$C, $-2.0\,\mu$C, $-2.0\,\mu$C sit at the corners of a $0.30$ m square, arranged so opposite corners carry the same sign.

**(a)** Find the net electric field **at the center**. (Do it with symmetry — no long arithmetic.)

**(b)** Find the electric potential at the center.

**(c)** Now find both $E$ and $V$ at the **midpoint of an edge** (e.g. between the two $+$ charges). *(This one you must compute.)*

## Problem 2 — The Dielectric Two-Step *(capacitors + dielectrics)*

A parallel-plate capacitor: area $0.010$ m², gap $1.0$ mm, air ($\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}$).

**(a)** Capacitance.

**(b)** Charged to $100$ V: find $Q$ and stored energy.

**(c)** A dielectric ($\kappa=3.0$) is slid in **while connected to the battery**. Find new $C$, $Q$, $U$.

**(d)** Instead, disconnect the battery **first**, then slide the dielectric in. Find new $V$ and $U$. Compare (c) vs (d) — in which case does the stored energy rise, and why? Where does the extra energy come from in (c)?

## Problem 3 — The Bridge That Measures *(circuits)*

A $24$ V battery feeds two parallel branches: branch 1 = $R_1(8\,\Omega)+R_2(4\,\Omega)$ in series; branch 2 = $R_3(6\,\Omega)+R_4(12\,\Omega)$ in series.

**(a)** Equivalent resistance and total current.

**(b)** Current through each resistor.

**(c)** Voltage across each resistor.

**(d)** Power in each resistor (check: sum = battery power).

**(e)** An ideal voltmeter connects the midpoint of branch 1 to the midpoint of branch 2. What does it read?

**(f)** What value of $R_4$ makes that reading zero (bridge balance)? Derive the balance condition $R_1/R_2=R_3/R_4$.

## Problem 4 — The Helix *(magnetic force, motion)*

A proton moves at $4.0\times10^6$ m/s at $30°$ to a uniform $B=0.20$ T.

**(a)** Find the magnitude of the magnetic force.

**(b)** The motion is a helix: decompose into $v_\parallel$ (along B, unaffected) and $v_\perp$ (circular). Find the radius of the circular component.

**(c)** Find the pitch of the helix (distance advanced along B per revolution).

**(d)** Does the magnetic force do any work on the proton? What does that imply about its speed and kinetic energy?

## Problem 5 — Coil in a Collapsing Field *(induction + Lenz)*

A $50$-turn coil of radius $0.10$ m has its plane perpendicular to $B=0.60$ T.

**(a)** Magnetic flux through the coil.

**(b)** The field drops to zero uniformly in $0.050$ s. Find the average induced emf.

**(c)** Instead of changing $B$, the coil is rotated $90°$ in $0.10$ s so its plane becomes parallel to $B$. Find the average emf.

**(d)** With the coil connected to a $10\,\Omega$ resistor, find the induced current in case (b), and state its direction by Lenz's law (field decreasing into the page — which way does current flow?).

**(e)** If the field collapses twice as fast, what happens to the emf and the current?
