## Problem 1 — Three Charges, One Balance Point *(Coulomb's law + stability)*

Two charges, $+q$ and $+4q$, sit $d=0.60$ m apart.

**(a)** Where between them does a positive test charge feel zero net force? (Distance from $+q$.)

**(b)** Would a **negative** test charge balance at the same spot? Explain.

**(c)** Analyze the stability: is this equilibrium stable against a small displacement *along* the line? *Perpendicular* to the line? (Call the point a saddle if it's stable one way and unstable the other.)

## Problem 2 — The Switch That Raises Resistance *(circuits)*

A $12$ V battery drives $R_1=6\,\Omega$ in parallel with $R_2=3\,\Omega$; that combination is in series with $R_3=4\,\Omega$. A switch $S$ is connected **across** $R_2$.

**(a)** Switch open: equivalent resistance, battery current, and the current through each resistor.

**(b)** Switch closed (shorts $R_2$): new equivalent resistance and currents.

**(c)** Compare the battery's power output and the power in $R_2$ in the two cases. *Explain the counterintuitive result: "shorting" a resistor raised the total resistance.*

## Problem 3 — Where Does the Energy Go? *(capacitors)*

A $C_1=6\,\mu$F capacitor is charged to $12$ V. A switch then connects it in parallel to an uncharged $C_2=3\,\mu$F.

**(a)** Initial stored energy.

**(b)** Final common voltage and final total stored energy.

**(c)** Charge is conserved, so why isn't energy? Compute the "lost" energy and say where it went. (This is *not* a violation of energy conservation — identify the mechanism.) What happens if $C_2\to\infty$ (a huge capacitor)?

## Problem 4 — The Velocity Selector *(E and B fields)*

A region has uniform $E=2000$ V/m (downward) and uniform $B=0.50$ T (into the page). A proton enters horizontally.

**(a)** Find the speed at which the proton passes straight through.

**(b)** If the proton is 10% faster, does it curve up or down? (State the direction of the magnetic force.)

**(c)** A deuteron (twice the mass, same charge) enters at the same speed. Does it go straight? Explain why the selector is independent of mass.

**(d)** After passing straight through, the proton enters a region with only $B=0.50$ T. Find the radius of its circular path.

## Problem 5 — Pulling the Rod Through a Field *(motional emf + power)*

A conducting rod of length $L=0.50$ m slides on frictionless rails (negligible resistance) in a uniform $B=0.40$ T field (perpendicular to the loop). The rails are joined by a resistor $R=2.0\,\Omega$. A constant external force $F=0.20$ N pulls the rod at constant speed.

**(a)** Find the terminal speed. *(Hint: at constant speed, external force = magnetic "drag" $BIL$, with $I=BLv/R$.)*

**(b)** Show that the mechanical power supplied equals the electrical power dissipated in the resistor.

**(c)** If $B$ is doubled, what happens to the terminal speed?

**(d)** If the rod itself has resistance $r=0.50\,\Omega$ (in series with $R$), find the new terminal speed.
