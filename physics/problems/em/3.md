## Problem 1 — The Floating Oil Drop

A charged oil drop of mass $2.5\times10^{-15}$ kg is suspended **motionless** between horizontal plates $2.0$ cm apart with $500$ V across them.

**(a)** Electric field between the plates.

**(b)** Magnitude of the drop's charge.

**(c)** How many excess electrons does that imply? Is your result consistent with the idea of discrete charge, and what does any small discrepancy tell you?

**(d)** If the drop instead carries exactly **5** excess electrons, what voltage suspends it?

## Problem 2 — Two Capacitors, Two Ways

$C_1=4\,\mu$F is charged to $100$ V; $C_2=6\,\mu$F is charged to $50$ V.

**(a)** Initial charge and energy of each.

**(b)** Connected **positive-to-positive** (parallel, same polarity): final voltage and total energy. How much energy is lost?

**(c)** Now connected **positive-to-negative** (opposite polarity): final voltage and energy. Why is the loss far larger?

## Problem 3 — Maximum Power, Minimum Efficiency

A battery of EMF $12$ V has **internal resistance** $r=2.0\,\Omega$. It drives an external variable resistor $R$.

**(a)** For $R=4\,\Omega$: current and terminal voltage.

**(b)** Find the value of $R$ that maximizes the **power delivered to $R$**, and that maximum power.

**(c)** Compute $P$ for $R=0.5,\,1,\,2,\,4,\,8\,\Omega$ and confirm your answer.

**(d)** At maximum power, what fraction of the battery's power is wasted in the internal resistance?

## Problem 4 — The Loop That Slides Into the Field

A rectangular loop (width $w=0.20$ m, height $h=0.40$ m, wire resistance $R=0.50\,\Omega$) is pulled at constant speed $v=2.0$ m/s **into** a region of uniform $B=0.50$ T (field into the page), entering edge-first along its width.

**(a)** While partially inside: induced emf, current, and the force you must apply to keep speed constant.

**(b)** Once **fully inside**, what is the emf and the required force?

**(c)** While **exiting**: repeat (a) — same or different?

**(d)** Check energy: power you supply vs. power dissipated in the resistor (during the entering phase).

## Problem 5 — The Magnet, the Tube, and the Solenoid

**(a)** A strong magnet is dropped through a long vertical **copper** tube and through an identical **plastic** tube. Compare the falls. Explain with Lenz's law, and identify the energy conversion.

**(b)** A solenoid: $200$ turns, length $0.40$ m, radius $0.020$ m, carrying $3.0$ A. Find $B$ inside (near center). ($\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$)

**(c)** The current is switched off in $0.010$ s. A small $10$-turn coil of radius $0.010$ m sits at the center, plane perpendicular to the axis. Find the average emf induced in it.

**(d)** Which way does the induced current in the small coil flow relative to the original solenoid current?
