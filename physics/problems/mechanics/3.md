## Problem 1 — The Satellite at 2R_E *(gravitation, escape)*

A satellite orbits Earth at radius $r=2R_E$ (altitude $=R_E=6.37\times10^6$ m). $G=6.67\times10^{-11}$, $M_E=5.97\times10^{24}$ kg.

**(a)** Orbital speed.

**(b)** Orbital period (in hours).

**(c)** Escape speed from that altitude.

**(d)** The satellite's speed is reduced by 2%. Describe qualitatively what happens to its orbit.

## Problem 2 — The Ambitious Roller Coaster *(energy + loop + friction + spring + collision)*

A $2.0$ kg cart is released from rest at height $H$ on a frictionless track. It enters a vertical loop (radius $R=3.0$ m), returns to a horizontal **rough** stretch of length $d=2.0$ m ($\mu_k=0.30$), and finally hits a spring ($k=300$ N/m).

**(a)** Find the minimum $H$ so the cart just completes the loop.

**(b)** Take $H$ twice that minimum. Find the maximum spring compression.

**(c)** Instead of the spring: a $1.0$ kg block sits at rest where the cart first reaches the rough stretch. The cart (arriving at $17.15$ m/s) collides and sticks. Find the combined speed right after, and how far the pair slides on a *long* rough surface before stopping.

## Problem 3 — The Yo-Yo That Teaches Rotation *(rotation + translation)*

A yo-yo is a solid disk ($M=0.20$ kg, $R=0.05$ m, $I=\tfrac12 MR^2$) with a light string wound around it. The string's free end is held fixed; the yo-yo is released from rest and unwinds (string does not slip).

**(a)** Find the acceleration of the yo-yo's center and the string tension. *(Combine the torque equation with the no-slip relation $a=\alpha R$ and Newton's 2nd law for the center.)*

**(b)** The yo-yo falls $1.0$ m. Find its speed.

**(c)** If it were a hoop ($I=MR^2$) instead, what would the acceleration be? Compare and explain.

## Problem 4 — Spring, Table, Cliff *(SHM/energy + projectile)*

A $0.50$ kg block is pushed against a horizontal spring ($k=800$ N/m), compressing it $0.20$ m, then released on a frictionless table. It slides off the table's edge, $1.25$ m above the floor.

**(a)** Speed when it leaves the spring (uncompressed position).

**(b)** Time of flight and horizontal landing distance.

**(c)** Speed just before it hits the floor.

**(d)** Now insert a rough patch ($\mu_k=0.40$, length $1.0$ m) between the spring and the table edge. Find the new landing distance.

## Problem 5 — The Conical Pendulum *(circular motion, Newton's laws)*

A $0.50$ kg mass on a $2.0$ m string swings in a horizontal circle with the string at $30°$ from the vertical.

**(a)** Find the tension in the string. (Vertical balance first.)

**(b)** Find the speed of the mass. (Radius $r=L\sin30°$.)

**(c)** Find the period of the circular motion.

**(d)** Qualitatively: what happens to the required speed as the string approaches horizontal ($\theta\to90°$)? Can it ever be exactly horizontal? Explain with your equations.
