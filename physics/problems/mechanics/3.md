## Problem 2 — The Ambitious Roller Coaster

A $2.0$ kg cart is released from rest at height $H$ on a frictionless track. It enters a vertical loop (radius $R=3.0$ m), returns to a horizontal **rough** stretch of length $d=2.0$ m ($\mu_k=0.30$), and finally hits a spring ($k=300$ N/m).

<details>
<summary><b>2.1</b></summary>

Find the minimum $H$ so the cart just completes the loop.

<details>
<summary><b>2.2</b></summary>

Take $H$ twice that minimum. Find the maximum spring compression.

<details>
<summary><b>2.3</b></summary>

Instead of the spring: a $1.0$ kg block sits at rest where the cart first reaches the rough stretch. The cart (arriving at $17.15$ m/s) collides and sticks. Find the combined speed right after, and how far the pair slides on a *long* rough surface before stopping.

<details>

<details>

<details>
