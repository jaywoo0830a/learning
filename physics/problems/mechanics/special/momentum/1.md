# Mechanics — Special: Momentum — Feel Builder

> **Purpose:** Five intuition-building problems on **momentum** ($p=mv$, impulse $J=F\Delta t=\Delta p$) before harder drills.
> **Level:** Regular Physics / SAT Physics — **algebraic only, no calculus.**
> **How to use:** Solve the folded sub-problems in order. Full solutions (✅ Solution) live in [`solutions/1-solutions.md`](solutions/1-solutions.md) — open a problem's solution only after finishing it.
> **The core feel to build:** momentum is always conserved in an isolated system, but **energy is not** — collisions trade momentum around while energy leaks out as heat, sound, and deformation.

---

## Problem 1 — The Egg and the Sheet

An egg ($0.10$ kg) falls from $2.0$ m onto (i) concrete, stopping in $2.0$ ms, or (ii) a stretched sheet, stopping in $0.20$ s. Take $g=10$ m/s².

<details>
<summary><b>1.1</b></summary>

Find the egg's speed just before impact, and the **impulse** needed to stop it.

<details>
<summary><b>1.2</b></summary>

Find the average force the surface exerts in **each** case (concrete vs sheet). Why does the sheet save the egg?

<details>
<summary><b>1.3</b></summary>

A $1200$ kg car hits a wall at $15$ m/s. With an airbag the stopping time is $0.10$ s; without, $0.010$ s. Compute the average force in both cases — the same principle as the egg.

</details>

<details>

<details>
<summary><b>3.2</b></summary>

<details>
