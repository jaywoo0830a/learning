## Problem 1 — From the Cliff, Two Angles *(projectiles, complementary angles)*

A stone is thrown from the top of a $20$ m cliff at $25$ m/s. Take $\sin37°=0.60$, $\cos37°=0.80$ (complementary $53°$: $\sin53°=0.80$, $\cos53°=0.60$).

**(a)** Thrown at $37°$ above horizontal: find the time of flight and the horizontal range.

**(b)** Find the speed and direction (angle below horizontal) at impact.

**(c)** Thrown at the complementary $53°$: find the range. Compare with (a). *Same speed, complementary angles — yet the ranges differ. Explain why the cliff breaks the usual "same range" rule.*

**(d)** Qualitatively: to maximize range from a cliff-top, do you launch above or below $45°$? Justify in one sentence.

## Problem 2 — The Crate That Won't Decide *(static/kinetic friction thresholds)*

A $10$ kg crate sits on a $25°$ incline ($\mu_s=0.45$, $\mu_k=0.30$; $\sin25°=0.42$, $\cos25°=0.91$). A force $F$ is applied **up** the incline.

**(a)** With no force applied, does the crate slide on its own?

**(b)** Find the minimum $F$ needed to keep it from sliding down.

**(c)** If $F=60$ N, does it slide up, slide down, or stay put? Find the friction force.

**(d)** Find $F$ for the crate to move **up** at constant speed.

## Problem 3 — Collision With a Choice *(energy + elastic/inelastic momentum)*

A $3.0$ kg block slides down a frictionless track from height $2.0$ m and collides on a level, frictionless surface with a $1.0$ kg block at rest. Afterward, the $1.0$ kg block slides up a frictionless ramp.

**(a)** Speed of the $3.0$ kg block just before the collision.

**(b)** If the collision is perfectly **elastic**, find both speeds after, and the max height reached by the $1.0$ kg block.

**(c)** If the collision is perfectly **inelastic** (they stick), find the max height. Compare with (b) and explain physically why it's lower.

## Problem 4 — The Falling Rod *(rotation + energy)*

A uniform rod (mass $3.0$ kg, length $L=2.0$ m) pivots without friction at one end. It is held horizontal and released.

**(a)** Find the torque about the pivot just after release, and the angular acceleration. ($I=\tfrac13 mL^2$)

**(b)** Using energy conservation, find the angular speed as it passes through the vertical.

**(c)** What is the speed of the **tip** at that instant? Compare it with the speed a point mass would have after falling freely through the same height $L$ — which is bigger, and why?

## Problem 5 — The Spring That Knows Its Amplitude *(SHM + energy)*

A $0.60$ kg mass hangs from a vertical spring and oscillates with amplitude $A=0.12$ m. Its **maximum speed** is $0.80$ m/s.

**(a)** Find the spring constant $k$. (Use $v_{max}=\omega A$.)

**(b)** Find the period.

**(c)** Find the total mechanical energy, and the speed when the mass is halfway between equilibrium and maximum displacement.

**(d)** If the mass is doubled (same $k$, same amplitude), what happens to the period and the maximum speed? Explain with $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$.
