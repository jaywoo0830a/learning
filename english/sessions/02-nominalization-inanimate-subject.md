# Session 02: 명사화·무생물 주어 해체 (단락 단위)

> **시간:** 60분 | **필드:** 수학·물리·화학·생물
> **오늘의 도구:** 단락 속 **명사화(-tion/-ment)**와 **무생물 주어·수동태**가 숨긴 '누가 무엇을 했는지'를 되살려, 각 문장을 행동으로 풀고 **단락 전체를 2~3줄로 압축**한다.

---

## 1. 시작 문제 (Opening Attack)

이 단락을 읽어보라:

> The measurement of the speed of light in a vacuum was performed by many scientists over several centuries. The refinement of the measurement by each generation reduced the uncertainty of the value. The establishment of a precise value for the speed of light was essential for the development of modern physics, because the speed of light appears in many fundamental equations.

문법적으로는 맞는데, "누가 뭘 했지?"가 계속 헷갈린다. 이유는 명사화(`measurement`, `refinement`, `establishment`)가 동사를 숨기고, 행위 주체가 문장 뒤로 밀려났기 때문이다.

이렇게 풀면 명확해진다:

> **단락 요약 (2줄):** 수세기에 걸쳐 여러 과학자가 진공 속 빛의 속도를 측정했고, 세대마다 측정을 다듬어 불확실성을 줄였다. 빛의 속도 값을 정확히 확립한 것은 많은 기본 방정식에 등장하기 때문에 현대 물리학 발전에 필수였다.

---

## 2. 실전 예시 (Worked Examples) — 단락 단위

### 예시 1 — 물리

**원문:**
The measurement of the speed of light in a vacuum was performed by many scientists over several centuries. The refinement of the measurement by each generation reduced the uncertainty of the value. The establishment of a precise value for the speed of light was essential for the development of modern physics.

**문장별 풀이 (명사화 → 동사구):**
- S1: 명사화 `measurement`←`measure`, 숨은 주체 `by many scientists` → **Many scientists measured the speed of light in a vacuum over several centuries.**
- S2: 명사화 `refinement`←`refine` → **Each generation refined the measurement and reduced the uncertainty of the value.**
- S3: 명사화 `establishment`←`establish` → **Establishing a precise value for the speed of light was essential for the development of modern physics.**

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (핵심 행동)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (측정 → 개선)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (S3은 '왜 중요한가' — 메모)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
수세기에 걸쳐 여러 과학자가 진공 속 빛의 속도를 측정했고, 세대마다 측정을 다듬어 불확실성을 줄였다. 빛의 속도 값을 정확히 확립한 것은 현대 물리학 발전에 필수였다.

### 예시 2 — 화학

**원문:**
The addition of a small amount of manganese dioxide to hydrogen peroxide causes the rapid decomposition of the hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen. The decomposition is accelerated because the manganese dioxide provides a surface on which the reaction can occur. The catalyst itself is not consumed during the reaction.

**문장별 풀이 (명사화 → 동사구):**
- S1: 명사화 `addition`←`add`, `decomposition`←`decompose` → **Adding a small amount of manganese dioxide to hydrogen peroxide makes it decompose rapidly into water and oxygen.**
- S2: 수동태 `is accelerated` — 원인은 이유절 → **The decomposition is accelerated because the manganese dioxide provides a surface on which the reaction can occur.**
- S3: → **The catalyst itself is not consumed during the reaction.**

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (현상)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (원인)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (S3은 핵심 메모)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
과산화수소에 이산화망간을 조금 넣으면 물과 산소로 빠르게 분해된다. 이산화망간은 반응이 일어날 표면을 제공해 분해를 가속하며, 촉매는 소모되지 않는다.

### 예시 3 — 수학

**원문:**
The proof of the theorem requires the use of the intermediate value theorem. The demonstration of the result proceeds in three steps. First, the existence of a solution is established. Then the uniqueness of the solution is shown. The verification of each step was completed by the student.

**문장별 풀이 (명사화 → 동사구):**
- S1: 명사화 `proof`←`prove`, `use`←`use` → **Proving the theorem requires using the intermediate value theorem.**
- S2: 명사화 `demonstration`←`demonstrate` → **The demonstration of the result proceeds in three steps.**
- S3: 명사화 `existence`←`exist`, 수동태 → **First, the existence of a solution is established.**
- S4: 수동태 → **Then the uniqueness of the solution is shown.**
- S5: 명사화 `verification`←`verify`, 숨은 주체 `by the student` → **The student verified each step.**

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (무엇이 필요한가)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (절차) → S5 (누가 했나)
- 버릴 것: S3·S4는 절차의 세부 (두 줄로 압축)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
그 정리를 증명하려면 중간값 정리를 사용해야 한다. 증명은 세 단계(해의 존재 확립 → 해의 유일성 증명)로 진행되며, 각 단계를 학생이 검증했다.

### 예시 4 — 생물

**원문:**
The production of insulin by the pancreas regulates the level of glucose in the blood. The secretion of insulin is triggered by an increase in blood glucose concentration. The binding of insulin to receptors on cells allows them to take up glucose from the blood. The failure of insulin production leads to the condition known as diabetes.

**문장별 풀이 (명사화 → 동사구):**
- S1: 명사화 `production`←`produce`, 숨은 주체 `by the pancreas` → **The pancreas produces insulin to regulate the level of glucose in the blood.**
- S2: 명사화 `secretion`←`secrete` → **An increase in blood glucose concentration triggers the secretion of insulin.**
- S3: 명사화 `binding`←`bind` → **Insulin binds to receptors on cells, allowing them to take up glucose from the blood.**
- S4: 명사화 `failure`←`fail` → **Failing to produce insulin leads to the condition known as diabetes.**

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (전체 기능)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (촉발) → S3 (작용) → S4 (결과)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (사슬 구조라 전부 핵심)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
췌장이 인슐린을 생산해 혈당을 조절하며, 혈당이 오르면 인슐린 분비가 촉발된다. 인슐린이 세포의 수용체에 결합하면 세포가 혈액에서 포도당을 흡수하고, 인슐린 생산 실패는 당뇨병을 일으킨다.

## 3. 방법 추출 — "방금 우리가 한 일"

```
① -tion/-ment/-ance/-sis로 끝나는 명사를 발견하면 동사구로 되돌린다
② 무생물 주어·수동태 뒤의 by ...에서 행위 주체를 찾는다
③ 각 문장을 '주체 + 동사 + 대상' 순서로 재구성한다
④ 재구성한 문장들을 이어 단락의 흐름을 만든다
⑤ topic sentence를 찾고, 핵심 문장만 남긴다
⑥ 단락 전체를 2~3줄로 압축한다
```

- **명사화 신호:** `-tion`(addition), `-ment`(measurement), `-ance`(dependence), `-sis`(synthesis)로 끝나는 명사가 동사를 숨기고 있을 가능성이 높다.
- **무생물 주어 신호:** `The results show...`, `The analysis reveals...` — 진짜 행위자는 사람(연구자·학생)일 때가 많다.

## 4. 기본 연습 (Basic Drills) — 단락

각 단락에서 ①명사화 ②숨은 행위 주체를 찾아 동사구로 풀고, ③topic sentence ④2~3줄 요약을 적어라. (풀이는 `solutions/02-solutions.md`)

### B1. (화학)
The oxidation of iron in the presence of oxygen results in the formation of rust. The combination of iron with oxygen produces iron oxide, which has a reddish-brown color. The prevention of oxidation is achieved by painting the iron or by coating it with another metal.

### B2. (물리)
The application of a constant force to an object causes its acceleration to remain constant. The increase in the velocity of the object continues as long as the force is applied. The removal of the force leaves the object moving at a constant velocity.

### B3. (생물)
The digestion of food in the stomach is aided by the secretion of gastric acid. The production of acid by cells in the stomach lining creates the acidic environment needed for the breakdown of proteins. The protection of the stomach wall from the acid is provided by a layer of mucus.

### B4. (수학)
The calculation of the integral was performed by the student using integration by parts. The choice of u and dv determines the difficulty of the calculation. The selection of an appropriate substitution often simplifies the evaluation of the integral.

### B5. (화학)
The determination of the pH of a solution is carried out using a pH meter or an indicator. The measurement of pH is important in many biological and industrial processes. The adjustment of pH is often necessary before a solution can be used in a reaction.

## 5. 심화 연습 (Advanced Drills) — 단락

명사화가 여러 개 겹치고 문장이 긴 단락이다. 전부 풀어 2~3줄로 압축하라.

### A1. (물리)
The conversion of kinetic energy into thermal energy occurs when two objects collide inelastically. The loss of kinetic energy during the collision appears as an increase in temperature of the objects. The measurement of the temperature change can be used to calculate the amount of energy converted. This principle is applied in devices such as automobile brakes, which convert kinetic energy into heat.

### A2. (생물)
The regulation of gene expression by the cell allows it to respond to changes in its environment. The activation of a gene leads to the production of a specific protein. The control of which genes are expressed determines the identity and function of a cell. The study of gene regulation is a central topic in molecular biology.

### A3. (수학)
The approximation of the value of pi by Archimedes using inscribed polygons was remarkably accurate. The construction of polygons with an increasing number of sides produced better and better approximations. The comparison of the perimeter of the polygon with the circumference of the circle gave bounds for the value of pi. This method anticipated the modern idea of a limit.

### A4. (화학)
The absorption of light by a molecule promotes an electron to a higher energy level. The difference between the energy levels determines the wavelength of light that is absorbed. The measurement of the absorbed wavelengths produces an absorption spectrum, which is characteristic of the molecule. The analysis of such spectra is used to identify unknown substances.

### A5. (생물)
The transport of oxygen in the blood is facilitated by the binding of oxygen to hemoglobin in red blood cells. The attachment of oxygen to hemoglobin occurs in the lungs, where the concentration of oxygen is high. The release of oxygen takes place in the tissues, where the concentration of oxygen is low. The efficiency of this transport system is essential for the survival of complex organisms.

## 6. 용어 정리 (Key Terms)

| 영어 | 한국어 | 메모 |
|------|------|------|
| nominalization | 명사화 | 동사를 명사로 바꾼 것 |
| inanimate subject | 무생물 주어 | 결과·측정·분석 등이 주어 자리 |

