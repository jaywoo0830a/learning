# Session 02 Solutions: 명사화·무생물 주어 해체 (단락 단위)

> 각 문항: **명사화→동사구** / **숨은 주체** / **topic sentence** / **단락 요약 (2~3줄)**

## Basic Drills

### B1. (화학)
The oxidation of iron in the presence of oxygen results in the formation of rust. The combination of iron with oxygen produces iron oxide, which has a reddish-brown color. The prevention of oxidation is achieved by painting the iron or by coating it with another metal.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `oxidation`←`oxidize`, `formation`←`form` → Iron oxidizes in the presence of oxygen to form rust.
  - S2: `combination`←`combine` → Iron combines with oxygen to produce iron oxide, which is reddish-brown.
  - S3: `prevention`←`prevent` → We prevent oxidation by painting the iron or coating it with another metal.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (철의 산화)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 산소가 있으면 철이 산화되어 녹(산화철)이 생긴다. 산화를 막으려면 철을 페인트로 칠하거나 다른 금속으로 코팅한다.

### B2. (물리)
The application of a constant force to an object causes its acceleration to remain constant. The increase in the velocity of the object continues as long as the force is applied. The removal of the force leaves the object moving at a constant velocity.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `application`←`apply` → Applying a constant force to an object keeps its acceleration constant.
  - S2: `increase`←`increase` → The velocity of the object keeps increasing as long as the force is applied.
  - S3: `removal`←`remove` → Removing the force leaves the object moving at a constant velocity.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (등가속도의 조건)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 일정한 힘을 가하면 가속도가 일정하게 유지되고, 힘이 작용하는 동안 속도는 계속 증가한다. 힘을 제거하면 물체는 등속 운동을 한다.

### B3. (생물)
The digestion of food in the stomach is aided by the secretion of gastric acid. The production of acid by cells in the stomach lining creates the acidic environment needed for the breakdown of proteins. The protection of the stomach wall from the acid is provided by a layer of mucus.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `digestion`←`digest`, `secretion`←`secrete` → The stomach secretes gastric acid to help digest food.
  - S2: `production`←`produce`, `breakdown`←`break down` → Cells in the stomach lining produce acid, creating the acidic environment needed to break down proteins.
  - S3: `protection`←`protect` → A layer of mucus protects the stomach wall from the acid.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (위산의 역할)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 위는 위산을 분비해 음식 소화를 돕는다. 위벽 세포가 만든 산이 단백질 분해에 필요한 산성 환경을 만들고, 점액층이 위벽을 산으로부터 보호한다.

### B4. (수학)
The calculation of the integral was performed by the student using integration by parts. The choice of u and dv determines the difficulty of the calculation. The selection of an appropriate substitution often simplifies the evaluation of the integral.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `calculation`←`calculate`, 숨은 주체 `by the student` → The student calculated the integral using integration by parts.
  - S2: `choice`←`choose` → Choosing u and dv determines how difficult the calculation is.
  - S3: `selection`←`select`, `evaluation`←`evaluate` → Selecting an appropriate substitution often simplifies evaluating the integral.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (무엇을 했는가)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 학생이 부분적분으로 적분을 계산했다. u와 dv를 어떻게 고르느냐가 계산의 난이도를 결정하며, 적절한 치환을 고르면 적분이 단순해진다.

### B5. (화학)
The determination of the pH of a solution is carried out using a pH meter or an indicator. The measurement of pH is important in many biological and industrial processes. The adjustment of pH is often necessary before a solution can be used in a reaction.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `determination`←`determine` → We determine the pH of a solution using a pH meter or an indicator.
  - S2: `measurement`←`measure` → Measuring pH is important in many biological and industrial processes.
  - S3: `adjustment`←`adjust` → We often adjust the pH before a solution can be used in a reaction.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (pH 결정 방법)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 용액의 pH는 pH 미터나 지시약으로 측정한다. pH 측정은 생물·산업 공정에서 중요하며, 반응에 쓰기 전에 pH를 조절해야 하는 경우가 많다.

## Advanced Drills

### A1. (물리)
The conversion of kinetic energy into thermal energy occurs when two objects collide inelastically. The loss of kinetic energy during the collision appears as an increase in temperature of the objects. The measurement of the temperature change can be used to calculate the amount of energy converted. This principle is applied in devices such as automobile brakes, which convert kinetic energy into heat.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `conversion`←`convert` → When two objects collide inelastically, kinetic energy is converted into thermal energy.
  - S2: `loss`←`lose` → The kinetic energy lost during the collision appears as an increase in temperature.
  - S3: `measurement`←`measure` → We can measure the temperature change to calculate the amount of energy converted.
  - S4: `principle` (명사) → This principle is applied in devices such as automobile brakes.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (비탄성 충돌의 에너지 전환)
- **단락 요약 (3줄):** 두 물체가 비탄성 충돌하면 운동 에너지가 열에너지로 바뀐다. 잃은 운동 에너지는 물체의 온도 상승으로 나타나며, 온도 변화를 측정해 전환된 에너지의 양을 계산할 수 있다. 이 원리는 자동차 브레이크 같은 장치에 적용된다.

### A2. (생물)
The regulation of gene expression by the cell allows it to respond to changes in its environment. The activation of a gene leads to the production of a specific protein. The control of which genes are expressed determines the identity and function of a cell. The study of gene regulation is a central topic in molecular biology.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `regulation`←`regulate` → The cell regulates gene expression to respond to changes in its environment.
  - S2: `activation`←`activate`, `production`←`produce` → Activating a gene leads to producing a specific protein.
  - S3: `control`←`control` → Controlling which genes are expressed determines the identity and function of a cell.
  - S4: `study` (명사) → The study of gene regulation is a central topic in molecular biology.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (세포의 유전자 발현 조절)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 세포는 유전자 발현을 조절해 환경 변화에 반응한다. 유전자가 활성화되면 특정 단백질이 만들어지며, 어떤 유전자가 발현되는지가 세포의 정체성과 기능을 결정한다.

### A3. (수학)
The approximation of the value of pi by Archimedes using inscribed polygons was remarkably accurate. The construction of polygons with an increasing number of sides produced better and better approximations. The comparison of the perimeter of the polygon with the circumference of the circle gave bounds for the value of pi. This method anticipated the modern idea of a limit.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `approximation`←`approximate`, 숨은 주체 `by Archimedes` → Archimedes approximated the value of pi using inscribed polygons, and it was remarkably accurate.
  - S2: `construction`←`construct` → Constructing polygons with more and more sides produced better approximations.
  - S3: `comparison`←`compare` → Comparing the polygon's perimeter with the circle's circumference gave bounds for pi.
  - S4: `method`/`idea` (명사) → This method anticipated the modern idea of a limit.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (아르키메데스의 근사)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 아르키메데스는 내접 다각형으로 원주율을 근사했고 그 결과는 놀랍도록 정확했다. 변이 많아질수록 근사가 좋아졌으며, 다각형 둘레와 원 둘레를 비교해 원주율의 범위를 얻었다 — 이는 극한의 현대적 개념을 예고했다.

### A4. (화학)
The absorption of light by a molecule promotes an electron to a higher energy level. The difference between the energy levels determines the wavelength of light that is absorbed. The measurement of the absorbed wavelengths produces an absorption spectrum, which is characteristic of the molecule. The analysis of such spectra is used to identify unknown substances.

  - S2: `difference` (명사) → The difference between the energy levels determines the wavelength absorbed.
  - S3: `measurement`←`measure` → Measuring the absorbed wavelengths produces an absorption spectrum characteristic of the molecule.
  - S4: `analysis`←`analyze` → Analyzing such spectra is used to identify unknown substances.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (빛 흡수와 전자 전이)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 분자가 빛을 흡수하면 전자가 더 높은 에너지 준위로 올라간다. 에너지 준위 차이가 흡수되는 빛의 파장을 결정하며, 흡수 스펙트럼은 분자마다 고유해서 미지 물질을 확인하는 데 쓰인다.

### A5. (생물)
The transport of oxygen in the blood is facilitated by the binding of oxygen to hemoglobin in red blood cells. The attachment of oxygen to hemoglobin occurs in the lungs, where the concentration of oxygen is high. The release of oxygen takes place in the tissues, where the concentration of oxygen is low. The efficiency of this transport system is essential for the survival of complex organisms.

- **명사화→동사구:**
  - S1: `transport`←`transport`, `binding`←`bind` → Hemoglobin in red blood cells binds oxygen, which facilitates the transport of oxygen in the blood.
  - S2: `attachment`←`attach` → Oxygen attaches to hemoglobin in the lungs, where its concentration is high.
  - S3: `release`←`release` → Oxygen is released in the tissues, where its concentration is low.
  - S4: `efficiency` (명사) → The efficiency of this transport system is essential for complex organisms.
- **topic sentence:** S1 (산소 운반의 핵심)
- **단락 요약 (2줄):** 혈액 속 산소 운반은 적혈구의 헤모글로빈이 산소와 결합함으로써 이루어진다. 산소는 농도가 높은 폐에서 결합하고 낮은 조직에서 방출되며, 이 운반 체계의 효율은 복잡한 생물의 생존에 필수다.
