# Session 03: 수식어 사슬 끊기 (단락 단위)

> **시간:** 60분 | **필드:** 수학·물리·화학·생물
> **오늘의 도구:** 단락 속 각 문장의 **전치사구 사슬**, **분사구(-ing/-ed)**, **관계절(which/that/whose)**이 꾸미는 대상을 찾아 골격을 만들고, **단락 전체를 2~3줄로 압축**한다.

---

## 1. 시작 문제 (Opening Attack)

이 단락을 읽어보라:

> The rate of change of the concentration of a reactant with respect to time is proportional to the concentration of the reactant. In many reactions, the rate of a reaction depends on the concentrations of the reactants raised to some power. The relationship between the rate and the concentrations is described by the rate law of the reaction.

`of`가 반복되고 수식어가 겹겹이 쌓여, "뭐가 뭘 꾸미지?"가 꼬인다. 이 세션은 각 문장의 수식어를 ( ) [ ]로 묶어 골격만 남긴 뒤, 단락 전체를 압축하는 법을 익힌다.

> **단락 요약 (2줄):** 반응물 농도의 시간에 대한 변화율은 그 농도에 비례한다. 반응 속도는 반응물 농도의 거듭제곱에 의존하며, 속도와 농도의 관계는 반응 속도 법칙으로 주어진다.

---

## 2. 실전 예시 (Worked Examples) — 단락 단위

### 예시 1 — 화학

**원문:**
The rate of change of the concentration of a reactant with respect to time is proportional to the concentration of the reactant. In many reactions, the rate of a reaction depends on the concentrations of the reactants raised to some power. The relationship between the rate and the concentrations is described by the rate law of the reaction.

**문장별 수식어 분석:**
- S1: 전치사구 사슬 → The rate **(of change)** **(of the concentration)** **(of a reactant)** **(with respect to time)**
- S2: 분사구 → ( `raised to some power` ) → 앞의 `concentrations`를 꾸밈
- S3: 골격 → The relationship is described by the rate law. (수동태)

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (핵심 관계)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (일반화) → S3 (이름 붙이기)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (S3은 용어 확정 — 메모)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
반응물 농도의 시간에 대한 변화율은 그 농도에 비례한다. 반응 속도는 반응물 농도의 거듭제곱에 의존하며, 속도와 농도의 관계는 반응 속도 법칙으로 주어진다.

---

### 예시 2 — 생물

**원문:**
The cells lining the small intestine absorb the nutrients released from digested food. These cells have finger-like projections called villi, which increase the surface area available for absorption. The blood vessels inside each villus carry the absorbed nutrients to the rest of the body.

**문장별 수식어 분석:**
- S1: 분사구 2개 → ( `lining the small intestine` ) → `cells`, ( `released from digested food` ) → `nutrients`
- S2: 분사구 ( `called villi` ) → `projections`, 관계절 [ `which increase the surface area available for absorption` ] → `villi`
- S3: 골격 → The blood vessels carry the absorbed nutrients.

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (흡수 기능)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (표면적 증가 장치) → S3 (운반)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (흐름이 연결됨)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
소장을 덮는 세포는 소화된 음식에서 방출된 영양분을 흡수한다. 이 세포에는 융모라 불리는 돌기가 있어 흡수 표면적을 늘리고, 각 융모 안의 혈관이 흡수된 영양분을 온몸으로 운반한다.

### 예시 3 — 수학

**원문:**
The function f, which is defined on the closed interval [0,1], attains its maximum value at some point in the interval. A continuous function on a closed interval always attains both a maximum and a minimum value. This result, known as the Extreme Value Theorem, is a consequence of the completeness of the real numbers.

**문장별 수식어 분석:**
- S1: 관계절 [ `which is defined on the closed interval [0,1]` ] → `f`를 꾸밈. 골격: The function f attains its maximum value at some point.
- S2: 골격 그대로 → A continuous function on a closed interval always attains both a maximum and a minimum value.
- S3: 분사구 ( `known as the Extreme Value Theorem` ) → `result`를 꾸밈

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S2** (일반 정리)
- 핵심 문장: S1 (구체 예) → S2 (일반화) → S3 (이름)
- 버릴 것: 없음

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
닫힌구간 [0,1]에서 정의된 함수 f는 그 구간의 어떤 점에서 최댓값을 갖는다. 닫힌구간의 연속 함수는 항상 최댓값과 최솟값을 모두 가지며, 이를 최대·최소 정리라고 한다.

### 예시 4 — 물리

**원문:**
The force acting on the block sliding down the inclined plane is the sum of the gravitational component and the frictional force. The component of gravity parallel to the plane pulls the block down the slope. The frictional force, which opposes the motion, acts up the slope. When the two forces are equal, the block slides at a constant velocity.

**문장별 수식어 분석:**
- S1: 분사구 2개 → ( `acting on the block` ) → `force`, ( `sliding down the inclined plane` ) → `block`
- S2: 골격 → The component of gravity pulls the block down the slope.
- S3: 관계절 [ `which opposes the motion` ] → `frictional force`를 꾸밈
- S4: 조건절 → When the two forces are equal, the block slides at a constant velocity.

**단락 골격:**
- topic sentence: **S1** (힘의 합)
- 핵심 문장: S1 → S2 (중력 성분) → S3 (마찰력) → S4 (평형 조건)
- 버릴 것: 없음 (사슬 구조)

**단락 요약 (2줄):**
경사면을 미끄러지는 블록에 작용하는 힘은 중력 성분과 마찰력의 합이다. 중력의 평행 성분은 블록을 아래로 당기고 마찰력은 위쪽으로 작용하며, 두 힘이 같으면 블록은 등속으로 미끄러진다.

## 3. 방법 추출 — "방금 우리가 한 일"

```
① 각 문장의 전치사구 사슬(of A of B of C)을 앞에서 뒤로 읽는다
② 분사구(-ing/-ed)가 꾸미는 대상(바로 앞 명사)을 찾는다
③ 관계절(which/that/whose)을 [ ]로 치고 골격만 남긴다
④ 각 문장의 골격을 한 줄 요약으로 적는다
⑤ 문장 요약들을 이어 단락의 흐름을 만든다 — topic sentence 찾기
⑥ 단락 전체를 2~3줄로 압축한다
```

- **분사구 판별:** `-ing`는 "~하는", `-ed`는 "~된"으로 해석하고, 그 앞의 명사를 꾸민다.
- **관계절 신호:** `which/that/whose/who/where/when`으로 시작하면 [ ]로 치고 골격을 먼저 본다.

## 4. 기본 연습 (Basic Drills) — 단락

각 단락에서 수식어가 꾸미는 대상을 찾아 ( ) [ ]로 묶고, topic sentence와 2~3줄 요약을 적어라. (풀이는 `solutions/03-solutions.md`)

### B1. (수학)
The length of the side of a square is equal to the diameter of a circle inscribed in the square. The area of the square is equal to the square of the length of its side. The area of the circle is equal to one quarter of pi times the square of the diameter.

### B2. (물리)
The speed of the car moving along the straight road increased steadily. The acceleration of the car, which was constant, was measured by the driver. The distance traveled by the car was recorded at regular intervals.

### B3. (생물)
The blood vessels carrying oxygen-rich blood away from the heart are called arteries. The vessels carrying blood back to the heart are called veins. The tiny vessels connecting arteries and veins are called capillaries, and it is here that the exchange of materials with the tissues occurs.

### B4. (화학)
The electrons in the outermost shell of an atom determine its chemical properties. Atoms with a full outermost shell, such as the noble gases, are very stable. Atoms that need one or two electrons to complete their shell tend to form bonds with other atoms.

### B5. (수학)
The point at which the derivative of a function is zero is called a critical point. The value of the function at a critical point may be a local maximum, a local minimum, or neither. The classification of the critical point is determined by the sign of the second derivative.

## 5. 심화 연습 (Advanced Drills) — 단락

수식어가 겹겹이 쌓인 긴 단락이다. 전부 풀고 2~3줄로 압축하라.

### A1. (물리)
The magnitude of the force exerted by a spring is proportional to the distance by which the spring is stretched or compressed. This relationship, known as Hooke's law, holds as long as the spring is not stretched beyond its elastic limit. When the spring is released, it returns to its original length. The energy stored in the stretched spring is equal to the work done to stretch it.

### A2. (수학)
The value of the integral of a continuous function over a closed interval can be estimated by the sum of the areas of the rectangles inscribed under the curve. As the width of the rectangles decreases, the estimate approaches the true value of the integral. This limiting process defines the definite integral. The connection between integration and differentiation is stated in the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

### A3. (화학)
The energy released when a chemical bond is broken is equal to the energy required to form the same bond. In a reaction, the total energy change depends on the difference between the energy needed to break the bonds in the reactants and the energy released when new bonds form in the products. A reaction that releases energy overall is called exothermic, while one that absorbs energy is called endothermic.

### A4. (생물)
The neurons transmitting signals from the brain to the muscles release chemicals called neurotransmitters at the junction between two cells. The neurotransmitters diffuse across the narrow gap between the cells and bind to receptors on the surface of the next cell. This binding triggers a new electrical signal in the receiving cell. The entire process allows rapid communication between the nervous system and the muscles.

### A5. (수학)
The slope of the tangent line drawn to a curve at a given point is equal to the value of the derivative of the function at that point. The tangent line, which touches the curve at exactly one point, provides the best linear approximation of the curve near that point. As the point moves along the curve, the slope of the tangent line changes, and the derivative function describes this change.

## 6. 용어 정리 (Key Terms)

| 영어 | 한국어 | 메모 |
|------|------|------|
| prepositional phrase | 전치사구 | of/with/in/at 등으로 시작, 앞 명사를 꾸밈 |
| participial phrase | 분사구 | -ing(능동)·-ed(수동)로 시작 |
| relative clause | 관계절 | which/that/whose... 로 시작 |
| critical point | 임계점 | 도함수가 0인 점 |
| villi | 융모 | 소장 표면의 돌기, 표면적 증가 |
| neurotransmitter | 신경전달물질 | 뉴런 사이 신호 전달 화학물질 |
| Hooke's law | 훅의 법칙 | F = kx |
| tangent line | 접선 | 곡선 위 한 점에서 곡선에 닿는 직선 |

